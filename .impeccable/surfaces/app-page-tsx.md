# Surface brief — app/page.tsx (Pasta Mia homepage)

Scope: single long homepage for Pasta Mia, a neighbourhood pasta trattoria & bar in Pocitos, Montevideo, Uruguay. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: diners in Montevideo choosing where to spend a lunch or evening. Job: understand what Pasta Mia is, browse the three services, book a table. Proof/content: authored trattoria photography, three service experiences each with an accordion of specifics (sample menu / house notes / bar list), invented-but-plausible contact + hours. Constraints: no backend — reservation form is static UI only; nav links smooth-scroll within the page.

Memorable moment: the page is one warm room in Pocitos, seen through the three services of a day — the midday, the dinner, the bar — not a grid of equal cards. Each service opens in place to reveal its specifics.

Unresolved: none. Brief pins structure (Il Tridente Positano layout) and a warm light palette.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One trattoria room in Pocitos across the three services of a day — midday, evening, late bar. Refuses the restaurant template of hero + equal menu cards + generic sticky booking bar; experiences are alternating editorial image movements with in-place accordions, not a card row.

OWN-WORLD: Warm light — cream surfaces (#fdfbf7 / #f7f0e4), espresso ink (#2c221e), warm brown-grey secondary (#6f5d51). Tuscan terracotta (#bc4749) as accent and text links; warm clay (#d08c6a) as the lighter accent. Playfair Display display to 5.5rem tracking -0.022em; Montserrat body/UI. Full-bleed food photography under cream gradients; white cards lifting off the cream ground.

STORY: The visitor sees a singular handmade-pasta trattoria, feels the family-kitchen warmth through imagery and quiet motion, expands the services that interest them, and books a table via a styled static mockup.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-screen photograph of fresh pasta, warm cream gradient settling over the lower third. Fixed cream-glass nav: "Pasta Mia" wordmark + terracotta accent dot left; links La Carta / La Casa / Reservas; right an outline "Reservá una mesa" pill. Hero copy anchored lower-left: terracotta tracked dateline "Trattoria de barrio — Pocitos", Playfair Display headline (light, over a darker-tan scrim) over two lines "Pasta fresca hecha a mano, / recetas de familia y vino.", one brown-grey subtitle line, a ghost "Ver la carta de pastas" link with a basil-sprig glyph. Faint scroll cue bottom-center.

FORM: Brief-pinned editorial restaurant homepage (Il Tridente Positano structure); #1 on the ordered list because the brief pins it. No concept-seed roll — the brief paints its own picture and names a site to mirror; the palette is warm light per explicit user instruction.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
