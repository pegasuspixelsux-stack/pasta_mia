# Surface brief — app/page.tsx (Puerto homepage)

Scope: single long homepage for Puerto, a seafood restaurant & bar in Punta del Este, Uruguay. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: diners and visitors to Punta del Este / La Barra choosing where to spend a day or evening. Job: understand what Puerto is, browse the three services, book a table. Proof/content: authored coastal photography, three service experiences each with an accordion of specifics (sample menu / house notes / bar list), invented-but-plausible contact + hours. Constraints: no backend — reservation form is static UI only; nav links smooth-scroll within the page.

Memorable moment: the page is one room on the main pier at Punta del Este, seen through the three services of a day — the midday, the dinner, the bar — not a grid of equal cards. Each service opens in place to reveal its specifics.

Unresolved: none. Brief pins structure (Il Tridente Positano layout) and, per the user's later instruction, a light airy palette.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One coastal room at Punta del Este across the three services of a day — midday, golden hour, late bar. Refuses the restaurant template of hero + equal menu cards + generic sticky booking bar; experiences are alternating editorial image movements with in-place accordions, not a card row.

OWN-WORLD: Marine dark — deep navy surfaces (#0d2a4d / #0a2140), cool near-white text (#f2f7fb), grey-blue secondary (#aec3d8). Light beacon-blue (#79c2e3) as accent and text links. Playfair Display display to 5.5rem tracking -0.022em; Plus Jakarta Sans body/UI. Full-bleed photography under navy gradients.

STORY: The visitor sees a singular seafood restaurant & bar, feels the maritime atmosphere through imagery and quiet motion, expands the services that interest them, and books a table via a styled static mockup.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-screen photograph of the day's catch at the pier, dark navy gradient settling over the lower third. Fixed navy-glass nav: "Puerto" wordmark + blue accent dot left; centered links El Mediodía / La Cena / El Bar; right an outline "Reservá una mesa" pill. Hero copy anchored lower-left: blue tracked dateline "Punta del Este — Uruguay", Playfair Display headline in ink over three lines "El mar, / la cena, / el cielo nocturno.", one grey-blue subtitle line, a ghost "Ver más" link with a compass glyph. Faint scroll cue bottom-center.

FORM: Brief-pinned editorial restaurant homepage (Il Tridente Positano structure); #1 on the ordered list because the brief pins it. No concept-seed roll — the brief paints its own picture and names a site to mirror; the palette was then redirected light by explicit user instruction, which pins over the roll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
